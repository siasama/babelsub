巴别英语（BabelSub）隐私政策

最后更新：2026-10-23

巴别英语（英文名 BabelSub）是一款运行在 iPhone、iPad 与 Mac 上的字幕与播放应用。本政策说明应用实际如何处理信息。

1. 我们不做什么

  • 不集成广告 SDK，不进行跨 App 跟踪，不出售个人信息。
  • 付费为 Premium 月度订阅、年度订阅，以及一次性永久解锁。订阅由 Apple 收取并自动续订；永久解锁由 Apple 收取一次，不自动续订。不出售按次次数包。
  • 我们不经手银行卡号或 Apple ID 密码。

2. 留在设备上的数据

你导入或缓存的音视频、字幕、转录结果、播放进度和应用设置，主要保存在本机。整集字幕由设备上的 Whisper 生成，音频不会为了转录而上传。系统「翻译」使用系统 Translation，待译句子不发到巴别英语的服务器。

你自行填写的 API 密钥保存在系统钥匙串。课程浏览器会把网站 Cookie 等数据放在单独的本机存储里。删除 App 会清除这些本机数据。

3. 会离开设备的数据

只有在你使用对应功能时才会发生：

  • Apple 购买与恢复：付款由 Apple 处理。应用通过 StoreKit 确认当前 Apple ID 是否拥有有效的月度订阅、年度订阅或永久解锁，以便打开 Premium。应用不保存支付凭证。
  • 目录搜索：搜索词会发给 Apple 的 iTunes Search 与 Lookup，用来返回播客目录。你自行配置 Podcast Index 后，搜索词会发给 https://api.podcastindex.org；密钥留在本机钥匙串。巴别英语的服务器不保存这些搜索记录。
  • 点词音标：英语词先查本机词典。本机没有音标时，这个词会发给 https://api.dictionaryapi.dev。
  • Whisper 模型下载：你可以另行下载模型。文件来自 https://hf-mirror.com 上的官方 ggml 权重。英语转录可以使用随包的 tiny.en 模型。
  • 名校公开课网页：课程条目在 App 内打开学校官方网页，视频不导入资料库。未启用课程浏览器中继时，页面由学校站点直接提供。课程页里的链接会留在这个页面中打开。系统浏览器不使用课程浏览器中继。
  • 基础中继与课程浏览器中继：Premium 包含这两项连通回退，你也可以填写自己的服务器。基础中继用于你主动打开的公开节目：直连在网络层失败时，该节目地址会经所选服务器再取一次。课程浏览器中继只用于 App 内的课程页，启用后课程页会经所选服务器传输。传输期间服务器能看到目标地址。当前配置不写访问日志，传输结束后不保留正文。默认不经这两项发送 AI 摘要，也不经它们下载语音模型。两者都不接管整机网络。
  • AI 云端摘要：你同意向应用显示的接收方发送文本，并逐次确认后，App 才会将已生成的字幕整理稿经 HTTPS 发送给该服务。音频和视频文件留在设备上。默认会员连接经 www.bable.top 的巴别英语网关转发至 DeepSeek，沙盒测试使用 sandbox.bable.top。网关持有上游服务密钥。使用自备密钥时，App 将整理稿直接发送给显示的接口。接收方按其政策处理文本。默认服务参见 DeepSeek 隐私政策。切换接收方时需重新同意。你可以在 AI 云端摘要设置中撤回同意，已发送的数据按接收方政策处理。
  • 系统分享：你通过系统分享导出的内容，由你选择的目标应用处理。

4. 权限

  • 后台音频：用于后台继续播放。
  • 相机与相册：仅在你主动扫描或从相册选择中继配置二维码时请求。不用于拍照或录像，也不上传照片。
  • 当前版本不请求麦克风。

5. 系统与内容

支持 iOS 18 或更高版本，以及 macOS 15 或更高版本。

目录中的播客、TED、公开课等内容来自公开 RSS、公开目录，或你自己导入的文件与链接。相关音频、封面、标题与节目说明的版权归原权利人。本应用不主张对这些内容的所有权。权利人如认为展示不当，请用文末方式联系。

6. 订阅

Premium 包括月度与年度自动续订，以及一次性永久解锁。订阅的扣款、续订和取消在 Apple ID 的订阅管理中处理。永久解锁不自动续订，也不会自动取消已有订阅。可在 App 内使用「恢复购买」。免费版对整集转录和字幕导出设有次数上限；激活 Premium 后这两项额度放开，并包含 AI 云端摘要的内置连接、基础中继和课程浏览器中继。字幕文件导入不限次。

7. 你的控制

你可以删除自定义 API 密钥，在设置里关闭基础中继或课程浏览器中继，并在课程浏览器中使用「清除浏览器数据」。删除 App 可移除本机数据。

8. 未成年人

本应用不以未成年人为目标用户，也不会有意收集未成年人个人信息。

9. 政策更新

我们可能更新本政策。更新后会在本页面发布最新版本，并修改上方日期。

10. 联系方式

BabelSub (巴别英语) Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-23

BabelSub (Chinese name 巴别英语) is a subtitle and playback app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. This policy describes what the app actually does with information.

1. What we do not do

  • We do not ship an advertising SDK, we do not track you across apps, and we do not sell personal information.
  • Paid access is a Premium monthly subscription, a Premium yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime unlock. Subscriptions are billed and auto-renewed by Apple. The lifetime unlock is a single Apple charge and does not auto-renew. There is no consumable credit pack.
  • We never see your card number or Apple ID password.

2. Data that stays on the device

Audio and video you import or cache, subtitles, transcripts, playback progress, and app settings stay primarily on the device. Full-episode subtitles are produced by Whisper on the device, and audio is not uploaded for transcription. System translation uses the system Translation framework, and the sentences being translated are not sent to BabelSub’s servers.

An API key you enter is stored in the system keychain. The course browser keeps website cookies and similar data in a separate on-device store. Deleting the app removes this on-device data.

3. Data that leaves the device

This happens only when you use the matching feature:

  • Apple purchases and restore: Apple processes the payment. The app uses StoreKit to see whether the current Apple ID has an active monthly subscription, yearly subscription, or lifetime unlock, so it can turn Premium on. The app does not store payment credentials.
  • Catalog search: Search terms are sent to Apple’s iTunes Search and Lookup to return podcast catalogs. If you configure Podcast Index, search terms are sent to https://api.podcastindex.org; the key stays in the on-device keychain. BabelSub’s servers do not keep these search records.
  • Word phonetics: English words are looked up in the on-device dictionary first. When that dictionary has no phonetic, the word is sent to https://api.dictionaryapi.dev.
  • Whisper model downloads: You can download additional models. The files are official ggml weights from https://hf-mirror.com. English transcription can use the bundled tiny.en model.
  • University course pages: A course entry opens the school’s official page inside the app. The video is not imported into the library. When the course-browser relay is off, the school site serves the page directly. Links on the page stay in that browser. The system browser does not use the course-browser relay.
  • Playback relay and course-browser relay: Premium includes both reachability fallbacks, and you can also enter your own server. The playback relay is for a public show you open: after a direct connection fails at the network layer, that address is fetched again through the server you selected. The course-browser relay applies only to course pages inside the app. While it is on, those pages travel through the selected server. During transfer the server can see the destination address. The current server configuration does not write an access log and does not keep the body after transfer. AI summaries and speech-model downloads do not use either relay by default. Neither relay takes over the device network.
  • AI cloud summary: The app sends existing transcript text over HTTPS after you consent to the displayed recipient and confirm the individual request. Audio and video files stay on the device. The default membership connection sends the text through BabelSub's gateway at www.bable.top to DeepSeek using a server-held key. Sandbox testing uses sandbox.bable.top. When you supply your own API key, the app sends the text directly to the displayed endpoint. The recipient handles the text under its own policy. See the DeepSeek Privacy Policy for the default provider. Changing the recipient requires fresh consent. You can withdraw consent in AI Cloud Summary settings. The recipient's policy applies to data already sent.
  • System share sheet: Content you export is handled by the app you choose.

4. Permissions

  • Background audio: keep playback going in the background.
  • Camera and photo library: requested only when you choose to scan a relay-configuration code or pick one from Photos. They are not used to take pictures or video, and photos are not uploaded.
  • This version does not request microphone access.

5. Systems and content

The app requires iOS 18 or later, and macOS 15 or later.

Podcasts, TED talks, and open courses in the catalog come from public RSS feeds, public catalogs, or files and links you import. Copyright in the audio, artwork, titles, and descriptions stays with the original rights holders. This app does not claim ownership of that material. Rights holders can contact us at the address below.

6. Subscriptions

Premium is a monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription, or a one-time lifetime unlock. Subscription charges, renewal, and cancellation are handled in the Apple ID subscription settings. The lifetime unlock does not auto-renew and does not cancel an existing subscription. Restore Purchases is available in the app. The free tier has a limited number of full-episode transcriptions and subtitle exports. Premium removes those two limits and includes the built-in AI summary connection, the playback relay, and the course-browser relay. Subtitle-file import is not limited.

7. Your control

You can delete a custom API key, turn off the playback relay or the course-browser relay in Settings, and use “清除浏览器数据” in the course browser. Deleting the app removes on-device data.

8. Children

This app is not directed to children, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The new text will be posted on this page, and the date above will change.

10. Contact

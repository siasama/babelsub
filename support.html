巴别英语（BabelSub）技术支持

最后更新：2026-10-18

巴别英语是一款运行在 iPhone、iPad 与 Mac 上的字幕与播放应用。你可以导入本地音视频，或粘贴直链与 RSS，在设备上生成整集字幕并跟读播放。

系统要求

  • iPhone、iPad：iOS 18 或更高版本。
  • Mac：macOS 15 或更高版本。

联系我们

功能异常、购买恢复或产品建议，请发邮件到 543443966@qq.com。也可以加入 QQ 群 1091158215。

请在邮件里写上：机型与系统版本、App 版本、Apple ID 是否与购买时相同（不必发送完整 Apple ID 或密码），以及问题发生时的操作步骤。我们通常在 3–5 个工作日内回复。

购买与恢复

  • Premium 包含什么：月度订阅、年度订阅，以及一次性永久解锁。不出售按次次数包。免费版可转录 10 次、导出 10 次；激活后这两项额度放开。字幕文件导入和实时字幕不限次。
  • 付了钱仍显示免费版：付款由 Apple 处理。请在 App 内点「恢复购买」，并确认当前 Apple ID 与购买时相同。同一 Apple ID 可在其他设备上恢复。确认已扣款仍无效时，请把订单时间发到上面的邮箱。
  • 取消自动续订：月度与年度默认为自动续订。请到系统「设置 › Apple ID › 订阅」中关闭。Mac 可打开 Apple ID 订阅页面。至少在当前周期结束前 24 小时取消，否则会续订。关闭后，当前已付周期内仍可使用。
  • 永久解锁：一次性购买，不自动续订，也不会自动取消已经在续订的月度或年度。若仍不想继续扣订阅费，请自行到系统订阅里关闭自动续订。

导入、播放与字幕

  • URL 无法识别：请粘贴直链音视频（如以 .mp3、.m4a、.mp4 结尾）或 RSS feed。普通网页、短链和需要登录的链接暂不支持。
  • 某一集下不下来：节目地址由源站提供，可能暂时不可用或已经失效。可稍后重试，或在详情页刷新 feed。
  • 有的节目很慢或打不开：源站繁忙、海外 CDN 延迟，或当前网络到源站不通，都会造成加载慢或起播失败。可以换网络、稍后再试，或播放已经缓存的单集。Premium 提供基础中继，也可在设置里填写自己的服务器。中继只拉取你主动打开的公开节目链接。
  • 转录很慢：首次加载模型，或设备性能较低时，耗时会增加。长节目请尽量保持应用在前台，并连接电源。
  • 名校公开课：课程条目打开的是学校官方网页，不把课程导入媒体库。网页打不开时，可在浏览器里重试，或用「在系统浏览器打开」。课程浏览器中的「清除浏览器数据」只清除该浏览器里的网站数据。

数据与隐私

音视频和转录结果主要留在本机，整集转录不上传音频。启用中继或课程浏览器代理时，你打开的公开链接会经对应服务器传输。AI 云端摘要只发送字幕文本：免费用户使用自己的密钥和地址；Premium 未改地址时使用内置连接。详情见 隐私政策。删除 App 会清除本机数据。

BabelSub (巴别英语) Support

Last updated: 2026-10-18

BabelSub is a subtitle and playback app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Import a local audio or video file, or paste a direct media link or RSS feed, and the app can build full-episode subtitles on the device.

Requirements

  • iPhone and iPad: iOS 18 or later.
  • Mac: macOS 15 or later.

Contact

For bugs, purchase restore, or product feedback, email 543443966@qq.com. You can also join QQ group 1091158215.

Please include the device and OS version, the app version, whether the Apple ID is the one used to purchase (do not send the full Apple ID or its password), and the steps that led to the problem. We usually reply within 3–5 business days.

Purchases and restore

  • What Premium includes: a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime unlock. There is no consumable credit pack. The free tier includes 10 transcriptions and 10 exports. Premium removes those two limits. Subtitle-file import and live captions stay unlimited.
  • You paid and the app still shows Free: Apple processes the payment. Tap Restore Purchases in the app and use the same Apple ID. That Apple ID can restore Premium on another device. If the charge is confirmed and Premium is still missing, email the time of the order to the address above.
  • Cancel auto-renew: Monthly and yearly plans renew automatically. Turn this off in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions. On a Mac, open the Apple ID subscriptions page. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Access continues until the period you already paid for ends.
  • Lifetime unlock: It is a one-time purchase. It does not auto-renew, and it does not cancel a monthly or yearly subscription that is already renewing. Turn that subscription off yourself in the system subscription settings if you do not want further charges.

Import, playback, and subtitles

  • A URL is not recognized: Paste a direct media link (for example one ending in .mp3, .m4a, or .mp4) or an RSS feed URL. Ordinary web pages, short links, and pages that require a login are not supported.
  • An episode will not download: The media address comes from the publisher. It may be temporarily unavailable or expired. Try again later, or refresh the feed on the detail page.
  • A show is slow or will not open: A busy publisher, a distant CDN, or a network that cannot reach the source can delay playback or make it fail. Switch networks, try later, or play an episode that is already cached. Premium includes a basic relay, and you can also enter your own server in Settings. The relay fetches only the public show links you open.
  • Transcription is slow: The first model load, or a slower device, takes longer. For a long episode, keep the app in the foreground and connect to power.
  • University courses: A course entry opens the school’s official page. It is not imported into the media library. If the page does not load, retry in the in-app browser or use Open in System Browser. “清除浏览器数据” clears only that browser’s website data.

Data and privacy

Audio, video, and transcripts stay primarily on the device. Full-episode transcription does not upload audio. When relay or the course-browser proxy is on, public links you open are fetched through that server. AI summary sends subtitle text only: free users supply their own key and address; Premium uses the built-in connection when no other address is entered. See the privacy policy. Deleting the app removes on-device data.
